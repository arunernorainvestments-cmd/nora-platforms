NORA Platforms, Inc. ("NORA Platforms", "we", "our", or "us") operates the NORA Platforms application and website at noraplatforms.com (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our Service. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, username, and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth — we never store plaintext passwords). If you sign up through a referral link, we record the referring user's identifier to credit referral bonuses.

1.2 Platform Connections

If you choose to connect a third-party content platform (YouTube, Twitch, X/Twitter, TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook), we collect and store OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, your platform username, and publicly available metrics such as follower count, subscriber count, and live viewer count. This data is used solely to compute your Heat Score and power market resolution. It is never shared with other users, never used for advertising, and never sold. You may disconnect any platform at any time from your profile settings, which permanently deletes the associated tokens and metrics from our systems.

1.3 Activity Data

We collect records of actions you take within the Service, including bets placed, markets created, token transactions (purchases, rewards, promotions, referral bonuses), promotions applied, badges earned, challenge progress, and streaks. This data is essential to the operation of the platform.

1.4 Payment Information

Token purchases are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not collect or store your credit card number, full card details, or any other sensitive payment information. We receive from Stripe a session identifier and a confirmation of payment completion, which we use to credit tokens to your account. Please review Stripe's Privacy Policy for details on how Stripe handles your payment data.

1.5 Usage and Technical Data

We may collect standard technical information such as your browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited within the Service, and timestamps of activity. This information is used to maintain security, diagnose issues, and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Compute creator Heat Scores and resolve prediction markets
  • Process token purchases and distribute betting payouts and rewards
  • Evaluate challenge and badge progress
  • Send in-app notifications relevant to your account activity
  • Credit referral bonuses to qualifying users
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access
  • Respond to your support requests and inquiries
  • Improve and develop new features of the Service
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share data with Supabase (database and authentication), Stripe (payment processing), Vercel (hosting), and Cloudflare (DNS and security) as necessary to operate the Service. These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Connected Platforms: When you connect YouTube, Twitch, X/Twitter, TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook, limited OAuth credentials are exchanged with those platforms as part of the authorization flow. We do not share your NORA Platforms data with those platforms beyond what is inherent in the OAuth handshake.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of NORA Platforms, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: If NORA Platforms is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice before your personal data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4. Public Information

Certain information is visible to other users or to the public by design. This includes your username, profile bio, creator category, Heat Score, connected platform names (but not tokens), badge collection, and market activity (bets and markets you participate in as a Market Maker). Do not include sensitive personal information in fields that are publicly visible.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information and activity data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal, regulatory, or fraud-prevention purposes. Token transaction records may be retained in anonymized form for financial record-keeping.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (TLS), row-level security policies on our database, and access controls that restrict which operations can be performed by authenticated users versus our internal service layer. OAuth tokens for connected platforms are stored server-side and are never exposed to other users or transmitted to your browser. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately at privacy@noraplatforms.com and we will take prompt steps to delete it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. We honor rights under both the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) to the extent applicable, including:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Opt-out of data sale: We do not sell personal data; no opt-out is required, but you may contact us to confirm this
  • Platform disconnection: Disconnect any linked social media platform at any time from your profile settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@noraplatforms.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.
